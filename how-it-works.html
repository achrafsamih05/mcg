Our 5-Step Sourcing Process

Clear milestones, clear deliverables, at every stage of your order.

1. Submit Your Request

Tell us what you want to import: product name, description, quantity, target budget, and any certs or specs you need.

2. Supplier Matching

We shortlist 3–5 verified factories in China, benchmark prices, and negotiate terms on your behalf.

3. Sampling & Approval

Get samples before you commit. Review materials, finish, and quality — adjust specs as needed.

4. Quality Inspection

Our team visits the factory for pre-shipment inspection and sends you a report with photos and video before goods leave China.

5. Global Shipping

We book freight, handle customs, and track your shipment from the Chinese port to your warehouse door.

After-Sales Support

Reorder with one click, manage warranty claims with suppliers, and keep a dedicated agent on your side.
